The Great Wave

Description

Under the Wave off Kanagawa, also known as The Great Wave, is a Japanese woodblock print created by Katsushika Hokusai around 1830 to 1832. The image shows a large wave rising over several boats while Mount Fuji appears in the distance.

I chose this object because it is a well-known piece of Japanese art. The size of the wave compared to the boats makes nature look very powerful. and we can see Mount Fuji in the background really small, which gives us some perspective.
